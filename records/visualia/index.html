Visualia

Listen

Visualia has nine electronic soundscapes, and each one corresponds to one of the fractal art images by the American visual artist Janet Parke, printed in the CD booklet.

Bruno Sanfilippo describes it as the first “composite” album he made in Spain after arriving in Barcelona in 2000. It combines small acoustic instruments with electronic equipment brought from Buenos Aires: a Kawai K4r synthesizer, an E-mu Proteus 2 and 3, and a Lexicon reverb.

Visualia is not available on streaming services and is exclusive to Bandcamp.
