Pianette 2

ListenBandcamp

Pianette 2 is a piano album of twelve compositions that follows Pianette and keeps the spirit of that series: dreams, the fantasy of mechanical toys and the essence of the circus.

Bruno Sanfilippo returns to his childhood and explores the magic of memory, innocence and boundless imagination. He began the album with the intention of evoking the wonder of those early years, a world of dreams in which mechanical toys came to life.

The release was accompanied by a music video made with Super 8 footage shot by his father, Nunzio Sanfilippo, in Martínez, Buenos Aires, in 1969. Those family images were a source of inspiration for the music.

The album was released on 14 March 2024 through ad21. Graphic design and music video are by Javier Guglielmi.
