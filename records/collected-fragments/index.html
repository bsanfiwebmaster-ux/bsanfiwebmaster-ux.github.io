Collected Fragments

Listen

Collected Fragments is an EP of seven pieces drawn from various projects, some recent and some not so recent. It includes works previously shared on Bandcamp, as well as recordings never before released there.

“À Milli Reworked” starts from an original composition by the Belarusian pianist Anastasiya Ihnatovich, with arrangements by Bruno Sanfilippo, included in the compilation Piano Whispers 01 released by ad21. Here it is reinvented with real strings by the composer Felipe Lima.

In “Danza de Reflejos Reworked”, the German composer Stefan Kraft reimagines a piece by Bruno Sanfilippo with a string arrangement. The EP was released on Bandcamp, where it is exclusive.

Released through ad21music
