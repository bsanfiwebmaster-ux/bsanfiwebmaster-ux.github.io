Inside Life

ListenBandcamp

Inside Life is an album of seven pieces for piano and electronics, with cello by Julián Kancepolski. Mariel Aguilar sings on track 3, “Camille”, a tribute to Camille Saint-Saëns, and Bruno Sanfilippo sings on track 7.

It was recorded at Onix II Studio in Barcelona in 2013 and 2014, and analog-mastered by Ian Hawgood in Tokyo. Artwork and layout are by Andy Ruggia, from Buenos Aires.

Released through ad21music
