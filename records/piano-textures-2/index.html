Piano Textures 2

ListenBandcamp

Piano Textures 2 is the second volume of the Piano Textures series, with six piano pieces by Bruno Sanfilippo, recorded and mastered at Onix Studio in Barcelona.

The Piano Textures series spans five albums, 42 compositions and more than four hours of music since 2007. It is considered one of Bruno Sanfilippo’s most representative bodies of work, and it concludes with Volume 5.

Released through ad21music
