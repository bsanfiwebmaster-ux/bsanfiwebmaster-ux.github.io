Claroscuro

ListenBandcamp

Claroscuro is an album of nine pieces for piano, some of them with strings. Bruno Sanfilippo plays piano, Manuel del Fresno cello and Pere Bardagí violin.

It was mastered at Splendour Mastering in Barcelona.
