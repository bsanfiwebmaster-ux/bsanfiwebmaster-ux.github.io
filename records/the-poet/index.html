The Poet

ListenBandcamp

The Poet is an album of eleven pieces for piano, cello and violin. Bruno Sanfilippo plays piano, Julián Kancepolski cello and Pere Bardagí violin. It was written and recorded at Onix II Studio in Barcelona.

It was released via 1631 Recordings and Decca Publishing, and analog-mastered in Tokyo.

Released through ad21music
