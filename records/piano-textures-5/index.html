Piano Textures 5

ListenBandcamp

Piano Textures 5 is the fifth and final volume of the series, with eleven compositions written and recorded by Bruno Sanfilippo at Onix II Studio in Barcelona. It was analog-mastered by Ian Hawgood.

A Lexicon unit contributed to the texture of the piano sound, together with field recordings and electronic atmospheres. The CD and LP editions were designed by Esther Cañas.

The Piano Textures series spans five albums, 42 compositions and more than four hours of music since 2007. It is considered one of Bruno Sanfilippo’s most representative bodies of work, and it concludes with Volume 5.

Released through ad21music
