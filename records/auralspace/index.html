Auralspace (Remastered)

ListenBandcamp

Auralspace is an electronic album by Bruno Sanfilippo as Onix, recorded in April 2009 at Onix Studio in Barcelona. He used a Korg Radias synthesizer, an E-mu Proteus 3, a Boss Giga Delay DD-20, various whistles and some nature atmospheres, including birdsong.

This remastered edition was prepared at Onix II Studio in 2025. Auralspace is not available on streaming services and is exclusive to Bandcamp.
