Unity

ListenBandcamp

Unity is an album of eight pieces written and recorded by Bruno Sanfilippo at Onix II Studio in Barcelona. The closing title track runs nearly eleven minutes.

It was released through ad21 and the Russian label Dronarivm, and analog-mastered by Ian Hawgood in Warsaw, on a classic SSL desk and a Studer A80 reel-to-reel.
