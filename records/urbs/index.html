Urbs (Reworked)

ListenBandcamp

Urbs is a combination of ambient textures and location-based field recordings. It was originally released only on compact disc by Hypnos Recording (U.S., 2012).

In 2023 Bruno Sanfilippo revisited it to reimagine it with his current creative approach, in the same Onix II Studio, in the mountains near Barcelona. It is released under the name Onix.

Released through ad21music
