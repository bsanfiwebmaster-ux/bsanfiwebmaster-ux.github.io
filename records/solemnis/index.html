Solemnis (Remastered)

Listen

Solemnis was composed and released through Bruno Sanfilippo’s own label, ad21, in Buenos Aires in 1998, the year the label was founded. It came out on compact disc, in 1,000 copies.

To create it he used an Alesis MMT-8 MIDI sequencer, a Kawai K4r synthesizer, an Ensoniq EPS 16+ sampler, E-mu Proteus 2 and 3 modules and an Alesis Quadraverb processor, as on the two previous albums, which are now discontinued. Today, he notes, that equipment is clearly vintage.

It was re-released, remastered, on 17 October 2019. Solemnis is not available on streaming services and is exclusive to Bandcamp.
