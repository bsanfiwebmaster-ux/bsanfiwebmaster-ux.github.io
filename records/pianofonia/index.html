Pianofonía

ListenBandcamp

Bruno Sanfilippo presents Pianofonía, a new sonic journey between intimacy and imagination.

Composed during the quiet winter months at his Onix II Studio, nestled in the mountains surrounding Barcelona and embraced by Mediterranean pine forests, Pianofonía was born from voluntary solitude and the search for a creative space removed from the noise and pace of urban life.

Across these eleven piano compositions, Bruno Sanfilippo reaffirms the artistic identity that has defined a career spanning more than three decades. With an expressive musical language and a subtle atmospheric dimension, the album unfolds a sonic universe where, at times, simplicity becomes a vehicle for remarkable expressive intensity.

Carefully conceived, Pianofonía offers a journey through a variety of emotional landscapes, alternating moments of introspection, contemplation, and luminosity. Its delicate minimalist arrangements find a natural balance between silence, resonance, and space, inviting the listener to an intimate and unembellished listening experience.

The album also features the creative collaboration of acclaimed violinist Doina Bora and talented cellist Ardie Son, whose performances enrich several of the works while blending seamlessly with Sanfilippo's pianistic universe.

More than a collection of pieces, Pianofonía offers a place to inhabit. A space where music imposes neither images nor answers, but instead opens pathways for each listener to discover their own inner landscape and allow the imagination to complete what the notes merely suggest.
