Suite Patagonia

Listen

Suite Patagonia is dedicated to the stories that the lands of Patagonia, in Argentina and Chile, have held: the cacique Sayhueque, the myth of the Patagonian giant, the colonists’ wonder at an unknown fauna, and the contact between the native peoples and Magellan’s men.

The album joins native instruments and aboriginal songs with the sounds of southern fauna, a European view of a time and a place, and countless stories and legends.

The digital album includes a PDF of the booklet, with photographs and Patagonian stories. It was re-released digitally on Bandcamp in June 2015 and is not available on streaming services.
