Colours Bloom

ListenBandcamp

Colours Bloom is the new EP by Italian-Argentine pianist and composer Bruno Sanfilippo, released through ad21music, the label devoted to reflective, neoclassical piano.

Its six piano compositions explore melancholy and calm. They were recorded on his Kawai RX-3 at Onix II Studio in Barcelona.

The EP continues Sanfilippo’s exploration of introspective music and offers a space for inner stillness.

Released through ad21music
