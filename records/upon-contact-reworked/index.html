Upon Contact Reworked

ListenBandcamp

Upon Contact Reworked is built around a single composition. It opens with Bruno Sanfilippo’s original version of “Upon Contact”, followed by reworkings by six artists: Francesco Giannico, Olan Mill, Leonardo Rosado, Jorge Haro, Quivion and Hior Chronik.

The original was recorded at Onix II Studio in Barcelona and mastered by Taylor Deupree in New York. The reworked tracks were mastered by Ian Hawgood in Tokyo.

Released through ad21music
