Piano Textures 1

ListenBandcamp

Piano Textures 1 opens the series with eight piano pieces recorded in night sessions on a 1923 Steinway. They are joined by strings, synthesizers and an Armenian duduk played by Vahan Zakarian.

A Lexicon reverb unit adds depth to the sound of the piano, along with field recordings and electronic atmospheres. The album was released on Bruno Sanfilippo’s own label, ad21music, in 2007, and remastered at Splendour Mastering in Barcelona in 2014.

The Piano Textures series spans five albums, 42 compositions and more than four hours of music since 2007. It is considered one of Bruno Sanfilippo’s most representative bodies of work, and it concludes with Volume 5.
