Subliminal Pulse

Listen

Subliminal Pulse was originally released on 12 April 2011 by the American label Spotted Peccary. Bruno Sanfilippo explains the idea in a sentence: with his electronic instruments he takes the universe’s “Subliminal Pulse” and tries to build a bridge between his inner pulse and the pulse of outer space.

The instrumentation is a Korg Radias synthesizer, an E-mu Proteus 3, a Boss Giga Delay DD-20, various samplers and some nature atmospheres.

The album is not available on streaming services and is exclusive to Bandcamp.

Released through ad21music
