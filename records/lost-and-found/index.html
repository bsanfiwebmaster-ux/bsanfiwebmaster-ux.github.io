Lost & Found

ListenBandcamp

Lost & Found gathers five pieces of Bruno Sanfilippo’s that had been scattered across other projects or kept in the studio archive.

“Peter” was his contribution to the Ambientblog 10th Anniversary Collection, released in 2015. “InTROpiano” belongs to the album InTRO (ad21, 2006). “Piano Texture Found” and “Solitario” belong to the EP Piano Texture Found, released in 2012 by the Italian netlabel Laverna. “What I Dreamed” was recovered from the hard drive of the studio’s recording computer.

All the pieces were written and recorded at Onix II Studio in Barcelona. The artwork was commissioned from Francisca Pageo.

Released through ad21music
